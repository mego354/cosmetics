# Senior E-Commerce UX Audit & Optimization Plan — LUMÉ Luxury Beauty (Egypt) ## Executive Summary LUMÉ has a visually stunning editorial aesthetic ("Warm Egyptian Editorial Luxury"). However, a critical UX evaluation highlights key friction points that hinder conversion, product discovery, and friction-free purchasing in the Egyptian e-commerce market: ### 1. Navigation & Search Discovery - **Issue**: The search bar in the header is passive and lacks autocomplete / trending queries / quick-view results. Category navigation links lack visual hierarchy, megamenu previews, or badge indicators (e.g., "جديد / New", "الأكثر طلباً"). - **Fix**: Upgrade header with interactive predictive search dropdown (instant search results with thumbnail, price, quick-add, and trending searches like "سيروم الهيالورونيك", "أحمر شفاه نيود"). Add structured category megamenu cues. ### 2. Product Cards (PLP & Homepage) - **Issue**: Product cards in editorial mode have muted CTAs (simple icons or text links) that require too much cognitive effort. Key conversion triggers (primary active ingredient, suitable skin type, tangible benefits, verified review counts, exact savings in EGP alongside percentage) are inconsistent. - **Fix**: Redesign product cards with: - High-visibility primary action button ("أضيفي للحقيبة" with micro-feedback). - Explicit benefit tags (e.g., "ترطيب عميق 72h", "تفتيح ونضارة"). - Clear price architecture: Current Price in bold EGP, Original Price strikethrough, and exact saving pill (وفري 170 ج.م). - Quick-view modal trigger and direct size/shade swatch selector on hover/tap. - Transparent Egyptian trust signal ("شحن سريع 24 ساعة للقاهرة والجيزة"). ### 3. Product Details Page (PDP) - **Issue**: Purchase CTA can get lost amongst editorial dossier copy; choice architecture between sizes/variants needs clearer value-per-ml or recommended badge; delivery estimation needs immediate governorate selector with realistic delivery date; return guarantee and Ministry of Health registration trust markers need higher visibility next to CTA. - **Fix**: - Sticky bottom purchase bar for quick access when scrolling. - Clearer value proposition for sizes ("الأكثر توفيراً" badge on larger volume). - Instant governorate delivery calculator with real-world realistic timelines (القاهرة والجيزة: غداً، الإسكندرية والوجه البحري: 48 ساعة، الصعيد والبحر الأحمر: 3-4 أيام). - Prominent Egyptian trust seals right below CTA: "دفع عند الاستلام مع حق الفحص قبل الدفع", "مسجل بهيئة الدواء المصرية", "استرجاع مجاني خلال 14 يوم". ### 4. Cart & Checkout Flow - **Issue**: Checkout steps and order summary in the 2-step flow need tighter visual hierarchy; address form needs Egyptian-specific validation fields (Governorate, District/حي, Building/عمارة, Floor/دور, Apartment/شقة, landmark); payment method selection needs clearer security indicators and transparent COD inspection reassurance. - **Fix**: - Clear progressive milestone indicator (الحقيبة > بيانات التوصيل > تأكيد الطلب). - Clear item breakdown with quantity increment, thumbnail, size variant, and price. - Transparent breakdown of Subtotal, Promo Discount, Nationwide Shipping (with free shipping unlocked threshold), and Total VAT included. - Reassured payment options: Cash on Delivery with open-box verification note; InstaPay / Vodafone Cash direct QR/Number verification; Meeza/Visa/Mastercard 256-bit secure gateway; ValU 0% interest 3-month installment pill. - Clear primary CTA button with lock icon: "تأكيد الطلب بمبلغ X ج.م (الدفع عند الاستلام)".
